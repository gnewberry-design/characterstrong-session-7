🌟 Session 7: Living Our Values

CharacterStrong-inspired advisory lesson • 50 minutes

💜 What matters to us becomes visible through what we do.

🎯 Targets📚 Vocabulary⏰ Activities🎮 Practice📝 Exit Ticket👩‍🏫 Teacher Notes

🎯 1. Learning Target

I can identify my personal values, explain why they matter, and show how my choices and actions reflect those values.

✅ 2. Success Criteria

  • Identify at least three personal values.
  • Explain how values guide decisions and relationships.
  • Distinguish saying a value from demonstrating it.
  • Analyze two everyday situations using values.
  • Write one specific action plan for the coming week.

❓ 3. Essential Question

How can we make sure our everyday actions reflect the values that matter most to us?

Think about: What are values? Why is it sometimes difficult to live by them? How can our choices affect others?

💖 4. Why Are We Learning This?

Our values are the principles that guide how we treat people, respond to challenges, and make decisions. Understanding them helps us build trust, develop character, and make responsible choices—even when no one is watching.

🤝 Respect

Listening when someone has a different opinion.

🧭 Honesty

Telling the truth when it is difficult.

💪 Responsibility

Following through on commitments.

⏰ 6. Lesson Activities — 50 Minutes

👋 Welcome & Warm-Up 5 minutes

Think of someone you admire. What value do their actions demonstrate? Share in Teams chat, aloud, or in a private reflection.

🧠 I DO — Teacher Modeling 10 minutes

Explain the difference between naming a value and living it. Model: “I value responsibility, so I arrive prepared, complete work, and ask for help.”

🤝 WE DO — Scenarios 10 minutes

Discuss the scenarios below. Which values are involved? What action would demonstrate them?

🌈 YOU DO — My Personal Values 15 minutes

Choose three values. Explain why each matters and describe one way to demonstrate each. Use the interactive reflection below.

🎯 Closure & Exit Ticket 10 minutes

Complete the four-question exit ticket. Connect your answer to a real action you can take this week.

🎮 7. Interactive Values Practice

Read each scenario, think about the best response, then reveal an example. There can be more than one thoughtful answer.

💬 Scenario A: A classmate is excluded from an online group activity.

Which values could help guide your response?

Kindness and inclusion: Invite the classmate into the activity and help them feel welcome.
📓 Scenario B: You forgot an assignment and are thinking about making an excuse.

What would it look like to live your values?

Honesty and accountability: Explain what happened, accept responsibility, and make a plan to finish.
🗣️ Scenario C: You disagree with someone during a discussion.

How can you stand by your opinion and still respect theirs?

Respect and empathy: Listen, explain your view calmly, and disagree without insulting the person.

📊 10. Demonstration of Mastery

Use four exit ticket questions, each worth 25 points. Evaluate accuracy, relevance, specificity, and clarity. Teacher-selected mastery benchmark: 80%. For students below the benchmark, provide feedback and reassessment opportunities. Review individual targets as well as the total score.

🧩 11. Differentiation

  • Support: Value word bank, sentence starters, extra processing time, and verbal responses as appropriate.
  • Extension: Discuss situations where honesty and kindness, or other values, appear to conflict.
  • Virtual access: Use Teams chat, Nearpod, Canvas, and optional breakout rooms. Do not require students to disclose private experiences.

👥 12. Targeted Small Groups

  • Targeted support: Identify values using concrete examples.
  • Developing: Connect named values to observable actions.
  • Extension: Analyze complex situations and competing values.

💻 13. Resources

CharacterStrong Session 7 theme; Microsoft Teams; Nearpod; Canvas; Google Forms; MyAMP.

🏡 14. Real-World Extension

Living My Values Challenge: Choose one value and practice it intentionally over the next seven days. Students may voluntarily share what they learned during the next Advisory.

📝 15. Teacher Reflection

Review warm-up participation, scenario responses, and exit tickets. Identify which students can name values, explain their importance, and connect them to actions. Plan reteaching and reassessment based on evidence of mastery.
